About Me

I obtained a Master's degree in Systems at Universidad Nacional de General Sarmiento, Argentina, in 2016. I completed my PhD studies in 2026 at Departamento de Computación, FCEyN, Universidad de Buenos Aires. My research interests are in the broad area of Software Engineering, but particularly in behavioral model inference for programs and smart contracts.
